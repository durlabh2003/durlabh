Kartify
Conversational AI Shopping Assistant · PM & AI Workflow
Pilot Benchmark

Researched and designed an AI shopping engine that parses product queries, clarifies preferences, and delivers transparent, side-by-side comparison tables with reasoning.

Observed Signals & Lab Benchmarks
  • Internal Usability Test (n=9, self-run): ~78% task completion rate on cross-category product discovery

CafeOS
Modular Operating System for Independent Cafes · Product Owner
Beta Pilot

Unified disconnected cafe operations — replacing 4 fragmented apps with a unified QR ordering, KDS, billing, CRM, and inventory platform.

Operator-Reported Pilot Signals
  • Kitchen ticket turnaround time reduced by ~31% across a 3-cafe beta pilot
  • Order transcription errors dropped by ~42% via digital KDS integration
  • 3 live cafes actively onboarded and serviced during the initial cohort
“Core learning: Busy cafe operators don't want richer feature menus — they need zero-latency workflows with minimal taps per transaction.”
FinMate
Gen-Z AI Personal Finance Assistant · Founding PM
Closed Beta / QA

Internal QA & Target Objectives
  • Internal QA benchmark: Time to first financial insight < 60 seconds
